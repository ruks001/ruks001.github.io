# Content Guide

This file is a checklist for replacing the placeholder content.

## Homepage

Keep the homepage short. Recommended content:

1. Name, role, affiliation, location, email.
2. One-sentence research identity.
3. A 2–4 sentence overview of your work.
4. Three main research areas.
5. Two or three recent news items.

Avoid putting your complete CV or publication list on the homepage.

## Research page

For each major research area, answer:

- What problem are you solving?
- Why does it matter for robotics/autonomous systems?
- What approach do you use?
- What platform/data/sensors/models are involved?
- What is the outcome or current status?

For selected projects, consider adding links for paper, code, video, demo, or dataset.

## Publications page

Use one card per publication. Recommended fields:

- Year
- Journal/conference
- Exact title
- Authors
- DOI / PDF
- Code or project page when available

Keep manuscript status accurate: under review, accepted, in press, preprint, etc.

## CV page

Recommended sections:

- Experience
- Education
- Technical skills
- Honors / awards
- Academic service / teaching (optional)

A web CV should be readable and concise. Link the full PDF rather than reproducing every detail.

## News page

Good news items include:

- Paper accepted/published
- Conference presentation
- Award/fellowship
- Internship/research visit
- New robot/demo/project release
- Competition result
- Dataset/code release

Use one or two sentences per item.
